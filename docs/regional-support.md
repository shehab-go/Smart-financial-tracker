# Regional Support & Localization

Smart Financial Tracker aims to provide world-class support for financial institutions in the Middle East, with a special focus on Yemen and the Gulf (GCC) countries.

## 🇾🇪 Yemen Digital Wallets (Upcoming)
We are actively working on adding regex presets for the most popular Yemeni digital wallets. Due to the unique financial landscape in Yemen, these wallets are essential for daily transactions.

### Target Wallets:
- **mFloos (Alkuraimi Bank)**: Support for POS payments (Hasseb) and P2P transfers.
- **ONE Cash**: Support for international remittances (MoneyGram) and merchant payments.
- **Mobile Money (CAC Bank)**: Support for utility and government fee payments.
- **Sabacash**: Support for telecom-based transactions.
- **Jawali (WeCash)**: Support for private digital transfers.
- **Shillen (Al-Qutaibi Bank)**: Support for digital entertainment and retail payments.

---

## 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia & GCC (Upcoming)
- **STC Pay**: Full support for purchases and transfers.
- **Urpay**: Support for digital wallet transactions.
- **Alinma / Al Rajhi**: Support for standard banking notification formats.

## 🤝 How to Help?
If you use any of these apps, you can help us by providing **anonymized** notification samples.
1. Capture a notification from the app.
2. **Remove any sensitive info** (Exact names, full account numbers).
3. Open an issue or a Pull Request with the text pattern.

Your contribution helps thousands of users track their finances more accurately!
